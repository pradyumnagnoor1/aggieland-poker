# Operating the Jokers queue

## Setup and opening

Log in with an individual staff account at /staff/login. Owners/managers approve Queue rules: arrival deadline, offer window, freshness interval. New settings apply to future entries/offers. Owners create staff accounts in Team and share temporary passwords privately. Staff changes passwords in My account.

Open a shift, which remains active across midnight until explicitly closed. In Games & seats, create/select an actual game, mark forming/running, and enable online joins. Add available seat labels, e.g. Table 1 · Seat 4. Do not list occupied seats as available. Confirm game status regularly; stale games stop accepting remote joins.

## During play

- Select a game in Wait-list. Entries show name, reference, source, time, and state.
- Add walk-ins/call-ins. Walk-ins are checked in immediately; phone requests have the arrival deadline.
- For an online arrival, verify their ticket/name and tap Check in.
- Offer seat reserves the selected available seat and displays the offer on the player's page.
- Player acknowledgment does not seat them automatically. Tap Confirm seated once physically at the table.
- When a player leaves, mark the seat free in Games & seats.
- More actions handles cancellations/no-shows with reasons. Managers can override priority with a recorded reason.

Queue rank follows join time; staff offers seats to physically checked-in players. No waiting-time guarantee is displayed. Expired entries rejoin at the back or contact staff. Late acknowledgment cannot revive an expired hold.

## Closing and recovery

Pause joins during interruptions. Finishing one game cancels its outstanding entries and releases holds. Closing a shift resolves all game queues. These are explicit staff decisions, not automatic midnight resets.

During outages, stop making seating assumptions, pause remote joins if possible, record a temporary manual queue, and contact the maintainer. Reconcile real occupancy and pending players before reopening.

Players must keep their status page open. Closed/suspended browsers can miss alerts. There are no automatic SMS/email seat notifications.
